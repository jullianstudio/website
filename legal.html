2. Privacy policy

Data controller

Stepwolf OÜ (details above) is the controller of the personal data processed through this website, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR). Data relating to clients and orders is also covered by clause 17 of the Terms and Conditions.

What we process, why, and for how long

  • Quote and contact requests (Tally forms, email): name, email address, message and project details. Purpose: answering your request, preparing a quote and performing the contract. Legal basis: pre-contractual steps and contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Kept for up to 3 years after our last exchange if no order follows; invoicing data is kept for 7 years under Estonian accounting law.
  • Audience measurement (Umami, self-hosted on our own server): pages viewed, referrer, country, device and browser type. Umami uses no cookies and does not store IP addresses; visits are counted with an anonymised identifier that is regularly reset. Purpose: understanding how the site is used. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Only aggregated statistics are kept.
  • Technical delivery: when you visit, your IP address is necessarily transmitted to the hosting provider (GitHub). Fonts and scripts are hosted on this website. Purpose: displaying the website and keeping it secure. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Embedded media: videos and players from YouTube and Spotify (portfolio videos are displayed via Embedly) are only loaded when you click on them. From that moment, these providers receive your IP address and may set cookies under their own privacy policies.

Recipients and transfers

Your data is never sold. It is only shared with the service providers listed above (Tally, GitHub and, when you play media, YouTube/Google, Spotify or Embedly) and, for clients, with our accountant and payment providers. Some of these providers are located in the United States; such transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
